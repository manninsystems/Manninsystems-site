Sound familiar?

If three or more of these ring true, it's worth a conversation.

  • Job cards are on paper, and the office never quite knows where each job has got to.
  • Material certificates sit in a filing cabinet when a customer asks for them.
  • Offcuts pile up in the corner with no record of what's there.
  • Galvanising and powder coating go out, and getting them back on time is a phone job.
  • Nobody knows whether a job made money until long after the invoice has gone.

What your system can do

Built around how your shop works, using your names for your products and processes.

Jobs by drawing and revision

Each job carries its drawings and revision, so the shop floor always builds the right one.

Progress at each stage

Cut, bend, weld and finish booked from a screen at each area, so the office sees where every job is.

Material with certificates

Heat numbers and certs linked to the jobs that used them, ready for the customer.

Offcut stock

Usable offcuts booked back with their size and grade, and offered up before new stock is cut.

Subcontract tracking

What has gone out for galvanising or coating, where, and when it is due back.

Job costing

Hours and material per job against the quote, so you learn which work pays.
