    Technical data

    What's under the bonnet of every Mannin system, whichever trade it's built for.

    Record keeping
    One append-only ledger for every stock movement. Balances are worked out from it, never typed in.
    Corrections
    Reversing entries, linked to the original, with the reason kept. Nothing is edited or deleted.
    Traceability
    A lot on every movement, from supplier batch to customer delivery.
    Recipes and routings
    Versioned. Each job is pinned to the version it ran with.
    Shop floor
    Touch screens at each line or machine, one job at a time, with operator sign-in by PIN.
    Access
    Roles for the office, stations and viewers. No per-user fees.
    Hosting
    London, with daily backups.
    Your data
