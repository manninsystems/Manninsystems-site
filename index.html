What your system can cover

Each system is built for one business, so it covers what you need and leaves out what you don't.

Orders and scheduling

Customer orders turned into jobs, with a clear view of what's running, what's next and what's late.

Materials and recipes

Blends, masterbatch and regrind ratios for each product, with material usage worked out per job.

Tooling

Dies, moulds and fixtures: where they are, what they've run and when they're due for maintenance.

Stock

Raw material, work in progress and finished goods, updated as work is booked rather than at month end.

Traceability

Which batch of material went into which job, for when a customer comes back with a question.

Reporting

Output, scrap and downtime in plain figures you can act on.

How it works

No big-bang switchover. You keep working the way you do now until the new system has proved itself.

  1. Talk it through

    A call or a visit to see how you work now: orders, materials, the spreadsheets and the whiteboard.

  2. Start with one process

    We pick the area causing the most trouble and build that first, around your products and your terms.

  3. Run it side by side

    The new system runs alongside your spreadsheets until you trust the numbers.

  4. Switch over

    Then you move across, with support on hand and room to add more areas when you're ready.
